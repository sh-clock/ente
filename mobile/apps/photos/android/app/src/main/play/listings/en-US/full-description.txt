Photos is a private, offline photo gallery. Your photos stay on your device, in a local vault that is end-to-end encrypted at rest - there is no account, no cloud, and nothing leaves your device.

On-device machine learning runs completely on your hardware:
- Magic search: describe a photo in plain words and find it
- Automatic face clustering and a People view
- Albums, favorites and trash with restore

Everything else you expect from a gallery is here: a fast grid, large thumbnails, video playback, and sharing that works offline.

No ads. No trackers. No subscription. Open source at github.com/sh-clock/ente.
